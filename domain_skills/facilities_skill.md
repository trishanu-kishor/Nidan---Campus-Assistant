# Domain Skill: Campus Facilities & Housing Operations (FACILITIES)

## Overview & Scope
This skill manages residence hall maintenance, room and venue reservations, recreation/gym access, dining services & meal plans, campus parking permits, and lost & found procedures.

---

## 1. Residence Hall & Dorm Maintenance

### 1.1 Maintenance Work Orders
- **Submit Work Order:** `https://facilities.campus.edu/fixit` (or FixIt Mobile App).
- **Categories Supported:**
  - **Plumbing:** Leaking faucet, clogged drain, low water pressure, toilet overflow.
  - **HVAC:** AC not cooling, heater malfunction, thermostat calibration.
  - **Electrical:** Blown fuse, malfunctioning power outlet, light bulb replacement.
  - **Furniture & Locks:** Loose bed frame, jammed wardrobe, electronic lock battery low.
- **Service Level Targets:**
  - Emergency (gas odor, flooding, spark): < 1 hour (call 24/7 hotline +1 (555) 019-4824 immediately).
  - Urgent (heating/cooling failure in extreme weather): < 6 hours.
  - Standard routine repairs: 24–48 hours.

### 1.2 Dorm Key & Card Access Lockouts
- Locked out of dorm room? Visit Residence Life front desk for temporary loaner key (free first 2 times per semester; $20 fee thereafter).
- Damaged / lost RFID keycard: Replacement available at Card Services ($25 fee).

---

## 2. Space Booking & Venue Reservations

### 2.1 Study Rooms & Collaborative Workspaces
- **Library & Student Union Study Rooms:** Bookable up to 7 days in advance for 2-hour slots via `https://spaces.campus.edu`.
- Maximum 4 hours per student per week. Instant cancellation if not checked-in within 15 minutes of reservation time.

### 2.2 Event Halls & Classrooms
- Student organizations (registered RSOs) may reserve auditoriums, classrooms, and outdoor quads via Event Planning Services (minimum 10 business days notice required).

---

## 3. Campus Recreation, Dining & Parking

### 3.1 Recreation & Fitness Center (Campus Gym)
- **Facility Hours:** Mon–Fri: 06:00 AM – 11:00 PM | Sat–Sun: 08:00 AM – 09:00 PM.
- Free entry with active Student/Staff ID.
- Lockers available daily for free (bring own padlock) or semester rental ($35).
- Intramural sports leagues and group fitness classes schedule: `https://rec.campus.edu`.

### 3.2 Campus Dining & Meal Plans
- **Meal Plan Portal:** `https://dining.campus.edu`
- **Dining Halls:** North Commons, South Buffet, Student Center Food Court.
- Dietary options: Dedicated Halal, Kosher, Vegan, and Allergy-Free stations available at North Commons.
- Meal plan changes permitted until the end of the 2nd week of the semester.

### 3.3 Parking Permits & EV Charging
- **Parking Portal:** `https://parking.campus.edu`
- **Permit Types:**
  - Resident Student (Lot R): $220 / semester.
  - Commuter Student (Lot C): $150 / semester.
  - Faculty/Staff (Lot A/B): $280 / semester.
- **EV Charging Stations:** 20 Level-2 charging ports across North & South Parking Garages (Free 4 hours max per session with active permit).

---

## 4. Campus Lost & Found

- **Central Lost & Found Office:** Campus Police & Public Safety Building (Room 104).
- Items turned in are logged with barcode and photo on `https://lostandfound.campus.edu`.
- Valuable items (laptops, phones, wallets, jewelry) retained for 90 days; unclaimed items donated to charity.
