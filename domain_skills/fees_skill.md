# Domain Skill: Bursar & Student Financial Services (FEES)

## Overview & Scope
This skill provides comprehensive rules, billing schedules, payment gateway options, installment EMI plans, financial aid disbursement, refund policies, financial holds, and tax documentation (Form 1098-T).

---

## 1. Tuition Billing & Payment Deadlines

### 1.1 Academic Term Deadlines
- **Fall Semester:** Tuition bill generated July 15; Payment due date is **August 15**.
- **Spring Semester:** Tuition bill generated December 1; Payment due date is **January 5**.
- **Summer Term:** Tuition bill generated April 20; Payment due date is **May 10**.

### 1.2 Late Fees & Financial Holds
- Accounts with unpaid balances exceeding $200 past the due date incur a **$100 late payment penalty**.
- **Bursar Registration Hold (BUR-HOLD):** Prevents class registration, grade viewing, and transcript release. Holds are automatically lifted within 2 hours of payment settlement.

---

## 2. Payment Methods & Installment Plans

### 2.1 Supported Payment Methods
- **Electronic Check (eCheck / ACH):** 0% transaction fee. Requires Routing & Account Number.
- **Credit / Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex):** 2.75% merchant convenience fee.
- **International Wire Transfers:** Processed via Flywire or Convera through student portal with real-time currency conversion and no hidden intermediary bank fees.
- **529 College Savings Plans:** Checks must be mailed to Campus Cashier's Office with Student ID in memo line.

### 2.2 Flexible 3-Part Installment Payment Plan (IPP)
- **Enrollment Fee:** $45 per semester (non-refundable).
- **Schedule:**
  - Down Payment: 34% due at enrollment (before term due date).
  - Installment 2: 33% due 30 days after start of term.
  - Installment 3: 33% due 60 days after start of term.
- Automatic recurring deduction is available via linked bank account.

---

## 3. Financial Aid, Scholarships & Waivers

### 3.1 Scholarship Disbursement
- Institutional merit awards and departmental scholarships apply directly against tuition charges 10 days before term start.
- External scholarship checks must be endorsed and delivered to Financial Aid Office (Welch Hall, Room 105).

### 3.2 Excess Funds & Refund Direct Deposit
- If total aid/scholarships exceed billing charges, the credit balance is refunded via **eRefund Direct Deposit** into student's personal checking account within 3–5 business days.
- Setup eRefund on Bursar portal (`https://bursar.campus.edu/erefund`).

---

## 4. Course Drop & Tuition Refund Schedule

| Withdrawal / Drop Timing | Tuition Refund % | Mandatory Fees Refund % |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Prior to 1st Day of Class** | 100% Refund | 100% Refund |
| **Week 1 – Week 2 (Add/Drop Period)** | 100% Refund | 0% Refund |
| **Week 3 – Week 4** | 50% Refund | 0% Refund |
| **After Week 4** | 0% Refund (No refund) | 0% Refund |

---

## 5. Tax Statements (Form 1098-T)

- Form 1098-T (Tuition Statement for IRS education tax credits) is published electronically on the Bursar portal by **January 31**.
- Requires valid SSN or ITIN on student file. If missing, submit Form W-9S to Registrar.
