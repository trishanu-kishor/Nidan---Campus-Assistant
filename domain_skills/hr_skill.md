# Domain Skill: Human Resources & Student Employment (HR)

## Overview & Scope
This skill governs student employment, Graduate Teaching/Research Assistantships (TA/RA), staff payroll schedules, timesheet management, tax compliance (I-9 / W-4), employee leave policies, and workplace grievances.

---

## 1. Student On-Campus Employment

### 1.1 Eligibility & Hour Limits
- **Enrolled Undergraduate Students:** Maximum **20 hours per week** during active academic terms (Fall / Spring). Up to **40 hours per week** during official university breaks (Summer / Winter).
- **International Students (F-1 / J-1 Visa):** Strictly capped at 20 hrs/week during school sessions by federal regulation. Must maintain valid SEVIS status and hold an active SSN or receipt notice.
- **Job Portal:** Browse and apply at `https://careers.campus.edu/student-jobs` (Handshake Integration).

### 1.2 Onboarding Requirements (Pre-Hire Checklist)
1. Complete **Form I-9** (Employment Eligibility Verification) within 3 business days of first work date. Must present unexpired original documents (Passport or State ID + SSN Card/Birth Certificate) at HR Central.
2. Submit **Direct Deposit Authorization** on Workday (`https://workday.campus.edu`).
3. Complete mandatory Campus Safety & Title IX training within 14 days of hiring.

---

## 2. Graduate Assistantships (TA / RA)

### 2.1 Appointments & Stipends
- **Appointment Contracts:** Issued on a semester or academic-year basis (20-hour standard appointment = 0.50 FTE).
- **Tuition Remission:** Standard 20-hr TA/RA positions include 100% base tuition waiver and 80% university health insurance premium coverage.
- **Stipend Disbursement:** Paid bi-weekly on the standard campus payroll cycle.

### 2.2 Duties, Overtime & Evaluation
- TAs must coordinate with course instructors before syllabus distribution.
- Maximum workload is 20 hrs/week; supplemental campus appointments require prior approval from the Graduate School Dean.

---

## 3. Payroll, Timesheets & Tax Forms

### 3.1 Payroll Cycles & Deadlines
- **Bi-Weekly Cycle:** Hourly student workers submit electronic timesheets via Workday every alternate Sunday by 11:59 PM.
- **Supervisor Approval Deadline:** Following Monday by 05:00 PM.
- **Pay Day:** Alternate Fridays. Funds deposited via direct deposit usually reflect by 06:00 AM on pay day.

### 3.2 Tax Documents (W-2 & 1042-S)
- W-2 statements are generated electronically in Workday by **January 31** annually.
- Non-resident international employees receiving tax treaty benefits receive **Form 1042-S** by **March 15**.

---

## 4. Leave Policies & Workplace Conduct

### 4.1 Sick & Bereavement Leave
- Student employees accrue 1 hour of paid sick leave for every 30 hours worked (up to 40 hours per fiscal year).
- Graduate assistants are eligible for up to 5 days of documented sick leave per semester with faculty advisor notification.

### 4.2 Workplace Grievances & Title IX
- Workplace discrimination, wage theft, or harassment reports can be filed confidentially at `https://hr.campus.edu/grievance` or by emailing `ethics-hotline@campus.edu`.
